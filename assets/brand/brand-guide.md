# Datara brand guide

## Signature

Primary line: **Datara — Know your data. Govern with confidence.**

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#111827` | Primary text and dark surfaces |
| Terracotta | `#D97757` | Actions, active states and highlights |
| Warm ivory | `#F5F2EB` | Page background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and readable contrast surfaces |

Use status colors only for meaningful success, warning and failure information. Avoid replacing the Datara palette with reference-product colors.

## Assets

- `datara-logo.jpeg` and `.png` preserve the developer's raster logo.
- `datara-logo.svg` is a showcase-specific, palette-aligned vector treatment.
- `favicon.svg` is a compact showcase icon.

Maintain clear space around the mark, preserve proportions, provide descriptive alt text, and do not imply trademark registration. The logo and brand assets were created by the developer and are covered by the repository license (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). Do not use them to imply endorsement.
